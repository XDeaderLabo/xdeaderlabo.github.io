01 / The concept

A platform shaped by its own identity.

XDL OS is being developed as a custom ROM project. The goal is to explore a tailored system experience while learning, building, and refining the software behind it.

◈

Independent direction

A project created and developed under the XDeader Laboratories name, with its own visual identity and goals.

⌘

Android foundation

Built around the Android open-source ecosystem and custom ROM development concepts.

✳

Continuous learning

Development is an iterative process: researching, building, testing, and improving one step at a time.
